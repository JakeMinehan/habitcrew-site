Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about building habits with your crew.

What is HabitCrew?

HabitCrew is a habit tracker for iPhone and Android built for doing habits with friends. You can share a habit with up to 4 other people, see everyone's progress in one place, chat in a group chat for each habit, and nudge teammates who haven't completed it yet. You can also track habits on your own.

Is HabitCrew available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. HabitCrew is on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android.

How many people can join a group habit?

A group habit can have up to 5 members, including you.

How do I invite friends to a group habit?

First add your friends by searching for their username in the app. Then invite them when you create a group habit. The habit's owner can invite more people later from the habit's Members screen.

How does group progress work?

Everyone in a group habit logs their own completions, and nobody's progress affects anyone else's. The Group Progress view shows each member's progress for the current day, week or month as a ring around their profile picture, and a graph compares everyone over the past 12 weeks (or 12 days for daily habits). Tap a member to highlight their line, or choose Group to see the whole group combined.

Does HabitCrew support weekly and monthly habits?

Yes. Every habit has a goal per day, per week or per month, such as 3 gym sessions a week or 2 books a month. You can also set a count for each period, such as 8 glasses of water a day, and partial progress is shown as you go.

Is there a group chat?

Yes. Every group habit has its own group chat for its members, so you can cheer each other on right next to your progress.

What is a nudge?

A nudge is a reminder you send to a teammate in a group habit. They get a notification such as "jake nudged you to complete Morning Run". You can nudge people you are friends with, once a day per person in each group habit, and only if they haven't already completed the habit.

Can I use HabitCrew on my own?

Yes. You can track solo habits as well as group habits, and you can mark a solo habit as private so others can't see it.
