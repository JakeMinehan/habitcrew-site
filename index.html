HabitCrew is a habit tracker for iPhone and Android where you and up to 4 friends share a habit, see each other's progress, and nudge each other to stay on track.

  • Track anything in groups up to 5 members (or solo!)
  • Compare progress to motivate each other
  • Weekly and monthly habits are supported as well

Track Group Habits

Habits stick when someone's counting on you. Share a habit with up to 5 friends and everyone's progress lands in one place, so a skipped session gets noticed and a good week gets seen.

  • Accountability you can't get tracking solo
  • A little friendly competition
  • See everyone's progress at a glance

Motivate Teammates with Chat and Nudges

Every group habit comes with its own group chat, so you can celebrate a 6am run or talk each other into tomorrow's, right next to your progress.

Someone falling behind? Send them a nudge and they'll get a reminder to finish the habit, straight from you.
